Hunger & Cravings

Some days you feel like you could eat the whole kitchen. Other days nothing sounds right. This is not a lack of willpower. This is your body talking — and it's worth listening to.

Before your period (week 3–4 of your cycle)

Progesterone rises, metabolism increases slightly, and your body craves carbohydrates and fats. This is real. Your body needs more energy in the second half of your cycle. Cravings for chocolate, bread, salty chips, or red meat are common. Not weakness. Biology.

During your period

Some people lose their appetite. Others feel hungrier than ever, especially for warm, comforting food. Both are normal. Your body is expending energy to shed the uterine lining — that takes fuel.

Puberty — growing requires fuel

Your body is building new tissue, laying down bone, changing shape. Feeling suddenly starving — even right after a meal — is common during growth spurts. It doesn't mean you're greedy. It means you're growing.

Emotional hunger

Stress, sadness, loneliness, boredom — all can feel like physical hunger. That doesn't make it fake. It just means food isn't always the answer. Sometimes you need rest, tears, or quiet.

🌙 A quiet note

Your body does not have a moral scoreboard. Eating when you're hungry — even if that hunger showed up for emotional reasons — is not a failure. You are allowed to eat. You are allowed to feel. Both can be true at once.

What helps (gentle, not a diet)

  • If you're craving something specific before your period — eat it. A little of what you want often stops the endless searching.
  • Pair cravings with something that gives longer energy — chocolate with a handful of nuts, chips with yogurt dip.
  • Hunger that feels urgent, shaky, or dizzy between meals might mean blood sugar dipped. Eating smaller, more frequent meals can help.
  • If you feel out of control around food often — or feel guilty every time you eat — that's worth talking to someone safe about. You deserve peace with food.

Cravings are not a sign that you're broken. They are often a sign that your body is doing exactly what it evolved to do — gather energy for the work of being alive.

Hunger is not your enemy. It is not shameful.
It is how your body asks for what it needs — and you are allowed to answer.
You don't have to earn food. You don't have to justify it. You just have to eat. 🌙
