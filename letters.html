To the girl who was never understood,

You learned early that some questions weren't welcome. That "why does my body do this" was met with silence, or a changed subject, or an answer that had nothing to do with what you actually asked.

So you stopped asking. You hid the weird feelings. You carried the confusion alone.

They gave you shame instead of information. They said "private" and made it sound like "secret."

But the silence was never about you. It was their discomfort. Their lack of words. You were not too much to explain. You were a girl who needed to understand — and someone should have met you there.

They didn't. And that was unfair.

Now you're here. Maybe still carrying some of that silence. Maybe still believing, quietly, that you are strange or broken or alone.

You are not. You never were. You were just never told.

And I want you to know: I understand. Truly. I know what it's like to wonder if your body is normal and have no one to ask. I know the weight of carrying questions you were made to feel ashamed of. I am not here to judge you or lecture you. I am here like an older sister — walking beside you, guiding you, telling you the things I wish someone had told me.

This space is yours. You don't have to perform here. You don't have to be smaller or easier. Arrive exactly as you are — and that will be enough.

You were a girl who wanted to be understood.

And here, you are.

With love,
Your older sister 🌙

You are not alone. You never were.
You just hadn't found this place yet.
Now you have. 🌙
