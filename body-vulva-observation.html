Vulva observation

Maybe you're just curious. You've never looked, and you want to know.
Maybe you've felt something different — a bump, a change — and you want to check.
Maybe someone said something that made you wonder if you're normal.
Maybe you're preparing for a doctor's visit.
Whatever brought you here: it's valid.

An invitation, not an assignment

There is no pressure. You don't have to do this today, or ever. This page is not a to-do list. If you feel nervous, that's normal. Read it once, put it aside, come back another day. No deadline. No wrong way to be here.

What you'll need

  • A small hand mirror (clean).
  • Soft, warm light — daylight or a lamp.
  • A quiet place where you feel safe.

One position — sitting on a chair

🪑 Sitting on the edge of a chair

Sit on a chair — a dining chair, a desk chair, anything firm. Scoot forward so your bottom is near the front edge of the seat. Let your knees fall open, feet flat on the floor or your toes touching. Place a small mirror on the floor between your feet, tilted slightly up toward you. Lean back just a little. Look down into the mirror. Adjust the mirror or your legs until you can see.

That's it. No contortions. No pressure.

What to notice (without judgment)

  • Outer lips: Texture, color, hair — just notice.
  • Inner lips: Tucked or extended? Symmetrical or not? Both are common.
  • Clitoris: The small nub at the top where the inner lips meet.
  • Color and markings: Darker areas, lighter streaks — that's anatomy, not a flaw.

✨ Most vulvas are not perfectly symmetrical. One inner lip longer than the other is extremely common. Your body is not a blueprint — it's a landscape.

What you might feel

Some people feel calm or curious. Others feel shy, awkward, or even sad. All of that belongs here. If you feel embarrassed, take a breath. You are undoing years of not being shown. That's not your fault.

Afterward

Set the mirror down. Breathe. Put your hand over your heart. Say (inside or out loud):
"This body is mine. It doesn't have to look like anyone else's."

No homework. No quiz. Drink water, rest, or go back to your day. Nothing is broken.
